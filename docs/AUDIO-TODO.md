## How each group should sound

**Words** — one word, said plainly, the way you would point at the picture
and name it. No sentence around it.

**Numbers** — just the number. The fives and tens are for a counting-in-tens
run, so they want the same even pace as one to ten.

**Letter sounds** — these are phonemes, NOT letter names: `sh` is "shh",
`ch` is "ch" as in chair, `oo` is the sound in moon. Saying the letter name
instead makes the blending game teach the wrong thing.

**Stretched blends** — a teacher sounding a word out slowly and then running
it together, e.g. `c-c-c... aaaaa... t-t-t... ccaaaatt`. Exaggerated and
unhurried; the dots are real pauses.

**Screen lines** — full sentences, read at normal storytime pace.
