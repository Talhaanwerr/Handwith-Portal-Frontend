==============================================================================
HOW EACH GROUP SHOULD SOUND
==============================================================================

WORDS
  One word, said plainly - the way you would point at the picture and name
  it. No sentence around it.

NUMBERS
  Just the number. The fives and tens are for a counting-in-tens run, so
  they want the same even pace as one to ten.

LETTER SOUNDS
  These are PHONEMES, NOT LETTER NAMES.
    sh  = "shh"            (not "ess-aitch")
    ch  = "ch" as in chair (not "see-aitch")
    oo  = the sound in moon
  Saying the letter name instead makes the blending game teach the wrong
  thing. The recordings in public/audio/phonics/ are the reference.

STRETCHED BLENDS
  A teacher sounding a word out slowly and then running it together:
    c-c-c... aaaaa... t-t-t... ccaaaatt
  Exaggerated and unhurried. The dots are real pauses.

SCREEN LINES
  Full sentences, normal storytime pace. The Picture Words line is read
  once at the start of every question, so it wants to be calm and short.
