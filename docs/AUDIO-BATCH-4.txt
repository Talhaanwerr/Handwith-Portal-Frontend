## Game Instructions

* Tap Play to start.
* Pick easy, medium or hard.
* Count them all. Then tap the number.
* Which number is missing?
* Drag the number to the empty spot.
* Pick a vowel to build with.
* Read the word. Drag it to its picture.
* Say each sound, then blend them together.
* The site is built!
* Pick a sorting game.
* Is it big or small? Drag it to the right box.
* Where does it live? Drag it home.
* Everything is sorted!
* Look at the picture. Tap the word that names it.
* Let's see how you did!
